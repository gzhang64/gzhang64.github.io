I work on biomedical informatics and natural language processing in the Weng Lab at Columbia. My research focuses on large language models and AI agents for clinical evidence synthesis, retrieval-augmented generation, and biomedical concept normalization. Before Columbia, I was a software engineer at Google Cloud, and I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Delaware.
