Gongbo Zhang

Associate Research Scientist · Department of Biomedical Informatics, Columbia University

I build large language models and AI agents for clinical evidence synthesis, retrieval-augmented generation, and biomedical concept normalization in the Weng Lab at Columbia University Irving Medical Center. Before Columbia, I was a software engineer at Google Cloud. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Delaware.

Research Focus

AI agents

Neural-symbolic agent pipelines for biomedical concept normalization, and self-corrective agentic mechanisms for question answering.

Retrieval-augmented generation

Long-context retrieval for medical question answering, and improving RAG systems without taxonomy-based error categorization.

Clinical evidence synthesis

Optimizing and deploying open-source LLMs for medical evidence summarization, with attention to trustworthiness.

Biomedical information extraction

Named-entity recognition for PICO elements in clinical trials, including overlapping entities and semi-supervised learning.
